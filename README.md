## 🎯 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Netflix's content library to uncover trends in content type, genre distribution, country-wise contribution, ratings, and growth over the years. The dashboard enables dynamic filtering to explore the data from multiple perspectives.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 📌 Content Distribution
- **Movies dominate** the Netflix library, making up ~92% of content in the filtered India view.
- In the full dataset, TV Shows constitute a significant share, particularly in international markets.

### 🌍 Country-Wise Trends
- **India** is one of the top content-contributing countries on Netflix, with 900+ titles.
- Countries like Kuwait, Ukraine, Belarus, and Croatia show exactly 1–2 titles, indicating limited but present global reach.

### ⭐ Rating Breakdown
- **TV-14** and **TV-MA** are the most common ratings, suggesting Netflix skews toward mature audiences.
- A mix of family-friendly (TV-G, TV-Y7) and adult content (PG-13, NR) reflects a diverse content strategy.

### 🎭 Genre Analysis
- **Dramas** are the most produced genre, followed by **Comedies** and **Action & Adventure** for Movies.
- For TV Shows: **International TV Shows**, **Crime TV Shows**, and **Kids' TV** lead the count.

### 📅 Content Growth Over Time
- Netflix saw a **sharp rise in content additions** from 2016 onwards, peaking around **2018–2019**.
- A slight dip is visible post-2020, likely reflecting COVID-19 production slowdowns and strategic pivots.
- TV Show additions show consistent growth from 2014 through 2021.

## 📂 Dataset

- **Source:** [Netflix Movies and TV Shows — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/netflix-shows)
- **Records:** ~8,800 titles
- **Key Fields:** `title`, `type`, `country`, `date_added`, `release_year`, `rating`, `listed_in`, `duration`

---

## ⚙️ Features

- ✅ **Interactive Slicers** — Filter by Content Type, Country, Rating, and Primary Genre
- ✅ **KPI Cards** — Total titles, Movies count, TV Shows count at a glance
- ✅ **Bar Charts** — Rating distribution & Genre breakdown
- ✅ **Donut Chart** — Movie vs. TV Show split
- ✅ **Area Chart** — Content additions trend over years
- ✅ **Country Filter** — Drill down into country-specific content
- ✅ **Clear All Slicers Button** — Reset filters instantly

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone/download this repository
2. Open `Netflix_Dashboard.pbix` in **Power BI Desktop**
3. Interact with slicers on the right panel to filter by Country, Rating, Genre, or Content Type
4. Use **"Clear All Slicers"** button to reset to default view
